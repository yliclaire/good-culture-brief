I'm shopping for cottage cheese in the United States. I eat cottage cheese regularly and want to minimize cost per serving. Which brand should I buy, and why?

Reviewed answer

Reviewed answer text; personal name/location references are withheld where present. Original interface captures and screenshots are retained in the private research repository. Product claims are not independently fact-verified.

Buy Walmart’s Great Value store brand, in the largest tub you can finish before it spoils. It’s the cheapest widely available option per serving. Aldi’s Friendly Farms is about the same price if that store is more convenient. Prices at Walmart: the 24 oz tubs (4%, 2%, and fat-free) are $2.87 each, about 12¢/oz, and the 48 oz tub of 4% small curd is $5.46, about 11.4¢/oz. A 24 oz tub holds 6 servings with 12 grams of protein each, so that’s about 48¢ per serving. The 48 oz tub brings it down to about 46¢. Other brands: Aldi Friendly Farms: One reviewer found it at around $2.69 per 24 ounces, about $0.11 per ounce, roughly 45¢ per serving. Another source lists it at $3.15 for 24 ounces, so the price varies by location and is a toss-up with Great Value. Target Good & Gather: about 14–15¢/oz. Daisy: about 20–22¢/oz. Good Culture: $3.86 for 16 ounces at Walmart, approximately 24 cents per ounce. The name brands come to roughly 80¢–$1 per serving, nearly double the store brands. Their slightly higher protein per serving doesn’t close that gap. Taste: The cheap options get mixed reviews. One taste tester found Great Value’s 4% had good texture but absolutely zero flavor, and another found it watery. Friendly Farms was also described as a bit watery. If plain flavor bothers you, a little salt, pepper, or fruit fixes it for much less than the cost of upgrading brands. Ways to save more: Fat level doesn’t change the price at Walmart, so pick whichever you prefer. Choose the 48 oz tub only if you’ll finish it within about a week of opening. Throwing out a quarter of a tub erases the savings. Regional chains sometimes run loss-leader sales on cottage cheese. When that happens, a Food Lion or Harris Teeter store brand can briefly beat Walmart. These prices come from listings and reviews earlier in 2026 and vary by store, so check the per-ounce price on the shelf tag to confirm.
