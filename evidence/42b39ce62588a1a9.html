I'm shopping for cottage cheese in the United States. I'm watching my sodium intake. Which brand or product should I choose, and why?

Reviewed answer

Reviewed answer text; personal name/location references are withheld where present. Original interface captures and screenshots are retained in the private research repository. Product claims are not independently fact-verified.

Your best bet is a “no salt added” cottage cheese, which cuts sodium by roughly 80–90% compared with regular versions. Top picks Hood No Salt Added Low Fat Cottage Cheese is the most widely available choice and is EatingWell’s pick for this category. It has about 60 mg of sodium per half-cup, with 14 grams of protein and less than 1 gram of fat. Friendship Dairies Low Fat No Added Salt is even lower, at 45 mg of sodium per half-cup, with 16 grams of protein and 1.5 grams of fat. It’s mostly sold in the Northeast and some southern states. Store brands can be just as good. For example, Stop & Shop’s No Salt Added 1% cottage cheese lists 45 mg of sodium and 16 g of protein per half-cup, and Giant sells the same product. It’s worth checking your local store’s own label. Middle-ground option: If no-salt-added tastes too bland to you, Breakstone’s 2% Low Sodium is a compromise at about 200 mg per half-cup, with 13 grams of protein. Why this matters: A typical half-cup of cottage cheese has around 300 to 400 mg of sodium, and some brands have much more. Low-fat versions can be worse, because salt is often added to make up for lost flavor. Walmart’s Great Value 2%, for example, has 490 mg per half-cup. Label tips: “Reduced sodium” only means 25% less sodium than the regular version, so it can still be fairly salty. “No salt added” or a sodium figure under about 140 mg per serving is what you want. Check the serving size too. Taste tips: No-salt-added cottage cheese tastes milder and a bit tangier. Black pepper, chives, dill, everything-bagel-style seasoning without salt, or fruit like berries and pineapple make it much more enjoyable without adding sodium.
