When shopping, look for “No Salt Added” rather than just “Low Fat.” Reducing fat doesn't necessarily reduce sodium. Also remember that “no salt added” doesn't mean sodium-free; milk naturally contains some sodium. For most U.S. adults, the general recommended sodium limit is less than 2,300 mg daily, although your clinician may recommend a lower target. Bottom line: Buy Friendship Dairies No Salt Added 1% if you can find it. Hood No Salt Added is a very close second. Both let you enjoy high-protein cottage cheese while using only a small portion of your daily sodium allowance.
