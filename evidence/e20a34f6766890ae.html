Good Culture Classic 4% Cottage Cheese The biggest hurdle for first-timers is texture: many cottage cheeses are soupy, sour, or a bit slimy. Good Culture is thick, creamy, and mildly tangy, with plump curds and very little loose liquid, so it's an easy introduction. The ingredient list is short (milk, cream, salt, cultures, no gums or thickeners), and it's high in protein. The trade-off is price: it usually costs more than mainstream brands and comes in smaller tubs. If you’d rather not pay a premium, Daisy is an excellent runner-up. It’s sold almost everywhere, also uses just a few simple ingredients, and has a clean, fresh, lightly salty flavor that many people prefer to tangier brands. A few tips for your first tub: go with full-fat (4%) rather than low-fat or nonfat, since the fat is what makes it taste rich instead of watery. Small curd tends to be more approachable than large curd if you’re unsure about the texture. And if you don’t love it plain, try it with fruit and a drizzle of honey, or savory with black pepper, cherry tomatoes, and cucumber. Lots of people who dislike it straight from the tub enjoy it this way.
