If you are watching your sodium intake, standard cottage cheese is notoriously high—often packing 300 to 400 mg of sodium per 1/2-cup serving. To drastically cut that down, you should look specifically for "No Salt Added" or "Low Sodium" labeled products, which drop the sodium content down to between 45 mg and 70 mg per serving. The top options to look for in U.S. grocery stores include: 1. Friendship Dairies "No Salt Added" Cottage Cheese Sodium Content: ~45 mg per 1/2 cup Why choose it: Friendship Dairies is widely available (especially in the Northeast and major natural grocers like Whole Foods). Their "No Salt Added" line slashes sodium to a fraction of regular cottage cheese while maintaining a solid protein profile. 2. Regional & Supermarket "No Salt Added" / "Low Sodium" Brands Hood "No Salt Added" Low Fat Cottage Cheese: Typically contains around 60 mg of sodium per 1/2 cup and is very common across the Northeastern and Eastern U.S. Store Brands (e.g., ShopRite, Kroger, Giant Eagle, and Martin's "No Salt Added" lines): Many regional grocery chains manufacture their own low-sodium versions boasting roughly 45 mg to 70 mg of sodium per serving. Breakstone’s "Low Sodium" Small Curd: Note that Breakstone's offers a specific Low Sodium version (which has about 45% less sodium than standard, usually landing around 200 mg per serving). While higher than "No Salt Added" options, it is a great stepping stone if you find completely salt-free cottage cheese too bland. Tips for Buying and Eating Low-Sodium Cottage Cheese Check the exact label: Make sure to distinguish between Reduced Sodium (which still might be higher than you want) and No Salt Added / Sodium-Free styles. Boost flavor naturally: Because salt is a primary flavor enhancer, unsalted cottage cheese can taste a bit flat initially. Try mixing it with fresh fruit (like pineapple, berries, or peaches), or add a salt-free seasoning blend (like Mrs. Dash or everything bagel seasoning without salt).
