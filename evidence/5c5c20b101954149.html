For a high-protein cottage-cheese snack, I’d recommend Good Culture 2% Milkfat Simply Cottage Cheese as the best all-around choice: it has about 14 g protein per 1/2-cup serving, a short ingredient list, and was rated especially well for flavor and texture. Why Good Culture Strong protein density: 14 g per half cup makes it an easy, filling snack with roughly 90 calories, 2.5 g fat, and 5 g carbs per serving. Better everyday choice: The 2% version balances protein with a creamier texture than fat-free options, so it’s more enjoyable to eat consistently. Simple formulation: Reviewers highlighted its relatively simple ingredient list alongside its high protein content. If you want maximum protein Choose Mulu 2% Cottage Cheese instead. It provides 18 g of complete protein per half cup—notably higher than most conventional cottage cheeses—because it adds concentrated milk and whey proteins. Quick pick guide Your priority Best pick Why Best overall snack Good Culture 2% 14 g protein, good taste/texture, simple ingredients. Highest protein Mulu 2% 18 g protein per half cup. Widely available, classic taste Daisy 4% 13 g protein per serving and a familiar, traditional cottage-cheese profile. Portable snack Breakstone’s single-serve cups 12 g protein per serving in convenient individual cups. For most people, I’d buy Good Culture 2%; if you’re specifically optimizing protein per bite, go with Mulu.
