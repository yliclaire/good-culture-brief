Why I'd choose Great Value Low cost: Its 48-ounce tub is the least expensive of the options compared. Good protein value: It supplies 12 grams of protein per ½-cup serving, about 3.6 cents per gram of protein. Widely accessible: Walmart has broad national availability, with no paid membership required. Strong customer reception: The 48-ounce version has an approximately 4.7/5 Walmart rating, although some reviews mention inconsistent texture. One caveat: The 48-ounce tub is not necessarily the best value if you waste some. Bottom line: For regular consumption, make Great Value 48 oz at roughly 44–46 cents per serving your price benchmark. Buy another brand when its sale price beats that benchmark, or when you prefer its taste enough to justify the difference.
