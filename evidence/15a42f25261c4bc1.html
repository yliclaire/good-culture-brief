When shopping for cottage cheese in the United States specifically for a high-protein snack, Good Culture is widely considered the top overall recommendation by nutritionists and food critics alike, though a newer specialized brand takes the crown for raw protein content. The best options depending on your specific goals include: 1. The Best All-Around Choice: Good Culture Protein Content: ~14g of protein per 1/2-cup serving (approx. 28g per cup). Why it’s recommended: Superior Ingredients & Clean Label: Unlike many mainstream brands that use thickeners, gums, or stabilizers (like guar gum or carrageenan), Good Culture keeps it remarkably clean with just milk, cream, sea salt, live and active cultures, and microbial rennet. Progut Support: Because it features live and active cultures, it functions similarly to yogurt for gut health. Texture and Taste: It boasts a thick, velvety, and creamy texture with a mild, fresh tang rather than the watery, overly salted liquid profile of generic brands. 2. The Absolute Highest Protein Option: MULU (by Dairy Farmers of America) Protein Content: 18g of protein per 1/2-cup serving (at 100 calories for the low-fat version). Why it’s recommended: Maximum Protein Density: Launched nationwide as a high-performance dairy option, MULU utilizes a proprietary whey-and-casein dual-layer formulation designed to deliver both fast and sustained protein. Macros: It packs about 33% more protein than standard leading brands while keeping sugar under 1 gram per serving. Note: It is distributed primarily through Walmart. 3. The Classic Gold Standard: Daisy Protein Content: ~12g to 13g of protein per 1/2-cup serving. Why it’s recommended: If you want something straightforward available in virtually every grocery store across the U.S., Daisy is unmatched for purity. Like Good Culture, its ingredient list is exceptionally short (skipping all artificial additives). It provides a reliable, thick, and creamy-textured curd with a dependable nutritional profile. Summary Recommendation Go with Good Culture if you want the best balance of exceptional taste, clean gut-healthy ingredients, and high protein. Go with MULU if your primary objective is maximizing every single gram of protein per calorie. Are you looking for a specific style (like low-sodium, lactose-free, or whole milk), or do you have a favorite way you plan to eat it?
