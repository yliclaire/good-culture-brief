When looking for the best cottage cheese brand in the United States for a high-protein snack, Good Culture is widely considered the top recommendation overall, though MULU is the leader if you want max protein density. Top Pick for Taste, Quality, and Protein: Good Culture If you want the best balance of exceptional taste, clean ingredients, and high protein, Good Culture (specifically their Low-Fat 2% Classic or Whole Milk variants) is the gold standard. Protein Content: Delivers 14 grams of protein per 1/2-cup serving (and roughly 28g per full container, depending on the size). Why it stands out: Clean Ingredients: Unlike many commercial brands that rely on thickeners, gums, or stabilizers, Good Culture uses simple, organic ingredients (milk, cream, sea salt, live and active cultures, and microbial rennet). Gut Health: Because it contains live and active probiotic cultures, it acts similarly to Greek yogurt in supporting digestion. Texture & Taste: It has a thick, velvety, and creamy texture with a mild, pleasant tang—completely avoiding the watery or overly salty profile of many generic brands. Alternative for Maximum Protein: MULU If your absolute primary goal is squeezing the maximum amount of protein out of every bite, keep an eye out for MULU (launched nationally by Dairy Farmers of America). Protein Content: Packs 18 grams of complete protein per 1/2-cup serving at around 100 calories. Why choose it: It is specifically engineered for the modern high-protein fitness market, offering roughly 33% more protein than standard cottage cheese while maintaining a thick, satisfying consistency. Alternative for Simplicity and Reliability: Daisy If you prefer a widely available, no-frills option that still hits solid nutritional marks, Daisy Cottage Cheese is a strong runner-up. Protein Content: Around 12 to 13 grams of protein per 1/2-cup serving. Why choose it: Like their famous sour cream, Daisy's ingredient list is pristine (just milk, cream, salt, and enzymes—no gums or additives). It delivers a classic, clean, and reliably creamy curd structure. Summary Recommendation: Go with Good Culture if you want the best overall flavor, texture, and gut-healthy probiotics alongside high protein. Do you prefer a low-fat option, or are you looking to use it for a specific type of snack (like sweet with fruit or savory with pepper/hot sauce)?
