For your very first cottage cheese purchase, I'd recommend Daisy 4% Milkfat Cottage Cheese (Small Curd). It's creamy, mild, reasonably priced, and a great introduction to what cottage cheese is supposed to taste like. Why I'd start with Daisy Mild flavor: It's fresh, slightly tangy, and a little milky-sweet. It isn't especially sharp or sour. Creamy texture: Soft curds surrounded by cream make it approachable, although you'll still notice the characteristic cottage cheese lumps. Full-fat tastes richer: The 4% milkfat version offers a more satisfying first experience than fat-free varieties. Simple ingredients: Just cultured skim milk, cream, and salt, without added gums or thickeners. Strong taste-test results: Daisy has performed well in multiple independent comparisons, including a recent Consumer Reports test that selected its 2% version as a top pick. I'd first taste a small spoonful plain, then add toppings. Cottage cheese has a mildly salty, creamy taste and a lumpy texture that's quite different from regular cheese or yogurt. Bottom line: Buy a 16-ounce container of Daisy 4% Small Curd. It's a low-risk starting point in both flavor and price. If you enjoy it but wish it were thicker and tangier, try Good Culture next.
