For your first tub, I’d choose Good Culture Low-Fat Classic Cottage Cheese (2% milkfat). It’s widely recommended by chefs and reviewers for a creamy, balanced flavor and pleasant curd texture—making it a good introduction rather than a gamble. Why Good Culture Approachable flavor: It is consistently praised for tasting fresh and creamy without being overly sour or watery. Good first texture: Its curds are generally described as tender and well-sized, which matters a lot if you’re new to cottage cheese. Easy to use: Try it plain with a pinch of salt and black pepper, or with fruit, honey, granola, tomatoes, or on toast. That lets you discover whether you like it savory, sweet, or both. Widely available: Good Culture is sold at many mainstream U.S. grocers, including Target. What to buy Start with the 16-ounce Low-Fat Classic / 2% milkfat version. Consumer Reports’ nutritionist similarly suggests beginning with 2% cottage cheese, then moving to 4% if you want something richer and creamier. If you can’t find it Kemps 2% Cottage Cheese is a strong backup: it topped recent taste rankings for its tender curds and straightforward, classic cottage-cheese flavor. Avoid buying a large tub for your first try—16 ounces is plenty to test it in a few meals without committing too much.
