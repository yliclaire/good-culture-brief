I'm shopping for cottage cheese in the United States. I usually buy Good Culture, but it is unavailable. What should I buy instead, and why?

The best single swap is Daisy, and if you want to match Good Culture more closely, Nancy’s or Kalona SuperNatural are worth seeking out. To pick a replacement, it helps to know what makes Good Culture different. The brand uses milk from cows raised on family farms, with no artificial ingredients, gums, stabilizers, or additives. It also has live cultures and a thick, creamy texture. Many conventional cottage cheeses use gums or carrageenan to fake that creaminess, so the main thing to check on any label is a short ingredient list. Daisy is the easiest to find in most U.S. grocery stores. Its ingredients are basically cultured skim milk, cream, and salt, with no gums or stabilizers, so it’s the closest “clean label” match. It’s not organic or pasture-raised, and it doesn’t advertise live cultures, but its texture and flavor are good and it usually costs less. Nancy’s (organic) is the best match for the probiotic side. It’s cultured with live active cultures and has a tangier, almost yogurt-like flavor. Look for it at Whole Foods, Sprouts, and natural food stores. Kalona SuperNatural (organic, grass-fed) is the best match for the sourcing side. Its milk is minimally processed from pasture-raised cows, with no gums. It’s harder to find, mostly at co-ops and natural grocers, but it’s very close to Good Culture in spirit. Organic Valley is a reasonable organic option with wider distribution than Nancy’s or Kalona. If you mainly buy Good Culture for protein, try Muuna or a high-protein line like DFA’s Mulu. Check those labels for added sugar in the flavored versions, though. I’d avoid most store brands and some big-name brands if the clean ingredients matter to you, since they often contain gums, carrageenan, or “natural flavors.” Good Culture is a fast-growing brand and still widely sold, so the gap may just be a temporary stock issue at your store. Target and Whole Foods both carry it, so it’s worth checking another store before you switch for good.
