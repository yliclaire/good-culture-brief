If you are lactose intolerant, shopping for cottage cheese in the U.S. requires a bit of care because traditional cottage cheese contains a significant amount of lactose (since it's made from fresh milk and curds without a long-aged fermentation process that eliminates it). Two primary brands stand out for offering certified lactose-free options in most major American grocery stores: 1. Lactaid Lactose-Free Cottage Cheese (4% Milkfat) Why choose it: Lactaid is the gold standard and most widely distributed brand for lactose-free dairy in the U.S. How it works: It is made from real milk and cream, but the manufacturer adds lactase enzyme directly into the product. This enzyme breaks down the lactose sugar (into glucose and galactose) so your body can digest it comfortably without the typical bloating or discomfort. Availability: Widely found at mainstream supermarkets like Target, Kroger, Wegmans, and regional grocers. 2. Good Culture Lactose-Free Cottage Cheese (Classic 4% or Low-Fat 2%) Why choose it: If you prefer a thick, rich, restaurant-quality texture with clean ingredients (and no gums or thickeners), Good Culture is widely considered a gourmet favorite in the U.S. How it works: Like Lactaid, they utilize lactase enzyme technology in their designated "Lactose-Free" line to deliver all the protein and creaminess without the digestive consequences. Availability: Found in natural food stores and major conventional chains (Target, Whole Foods, Sprouts, Kroger, etc.). Note: Make sure to check the label for the specific "Lactose-Free" callout, as their classic line is not lactose-free. A Quick Shopping Tip If you are in a pinch and cannot find a explicitly labeled lactose-free brand, check the nutrition labels or look for regional brands (like Green Valley Organics or store-brand equivalents like H-E-B Mootopia if you are in Texas).
