If you are shopping for cottage cheese in the United States and are lactose intolerant, you have a couple of fantastic, widely available options. Because traditional cottage cheese is packed with whey and milk solids, it naturally contains a significant amount of lactose. The top brands to look for on grocery shelves include: 1. Lactaid Lactose-Free Cottage Cheese (Best Overall / Most Widely Available) Why choose it: Lactaid is the household name for lactose-free dairy. Their cottage cheese (typically 4% milkfat) is made with real milk and cream, but they add the lactase enzyme to break down the lactose for you. Taste and Texture: It tastes remarkably close to conventional cottage cheese—creamy, rich, and traditional—without triggering digestive discomfort. Where to find it: Major nationwide supermarkets and grocery chains (e.g., Target, Kroger, Walmart, Publix, Safeway). 2. Green Valley Creamery Organic Lactose-Free Cottage Cheese (Best Organic Option) Why choose it: If you prefer certified organic dairy, Green Valley Creamery makes an excellent option. Like Lactaid, it uses real organic milk and cream coupled with the lactase enzyme, and it features live, active cultures. It is also certified humane and gluten-free. Taste and Texture: It has a clean, wholesome dairy flavor with a nice, traditional curd structure. Where to find it: Natural food stores and upscale grocers, including Whole Foods Market and Sprouts. A Quick Tip on Naturally Lower-Lactose Alternatives If you cannot find a explicitly labeled lactose-free brand near you, some people with mild lactose intolerance tolerate certain standard cottage cheeses better than others due to the manufacturing process: The Curd-to-Whey Ratio: Cottage cheese is strained, and much of the liquid whey (which contains most of the milk's lactose) is washed away. Live Cultures: Brands that boast live and active cultures (like Good Culture) contain beneficial bacteria that can assist slightly with digestion, though they are not officially lactose-free. Recommendation: For guaranteed comfort, stick to Lactaid or Green Valley Creamery so you can enjoy cottage cheese completely symptom-free.
