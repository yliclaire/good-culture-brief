My recommendation is Daisy 4% Cottage Cheese. It has one of the simplest ingredient lists on the U.S. market, is relatively affordable, and doesn't contain unnecessary thickeners or preservatives. If you prefer organic dairy, I'd choose Good Culture Organic 4% instead. My top 3 picks 1. Daisy Pure & Natural 4% Cottage Cheese Best overall for simplicity Ingredients: Cultured skim milk, cream, salt. Just three listed ingredients, with live cultures from fermentation. No gums, starches, or added preservatives. A good everyday choice. Example price: $3.97 / 16 oz at Walmart 2. Good Culture Organic 4% Cottage Cheese Best organic option Ingredients: Organic skim milk, organic whole milk, organic cream, sea salt, live and active cultures. No gums or starches, with organic dairy from pasture-raised cows. Thick, creamy texture and 14 grams of protein per half-cup. 3. Kalona SuperNatural Organic Whole Milk Cottage Cheese Best for traditional dairy production Ingredients: Organic nonfat milk, organic whole milk, organic cream, Celtic sea salt, cultures and a listed enzyme. Grass-fed dairy, non-homogenized, and no stabilizers or preservatives. Worth considering if farming practices are especially important to you. What matters on the ingredient label For plain cottage cheese, a great ingredient list is simply milk, cream, salt, and cultures. I'd favor products without added carrageenan, guar gum, modified food starch, artificial flavors, or unnecessary sweeteners. These additives aren't automatically harmful, but they're unnecessary if your priority is a short ingredient list. One nutritional consideration: even minimally processed cottage cheese can be fairly salty. For example, Good Culture Organic 4% has 380 mg sodium per half-cup serving. Final verdict Choose Daisy 4% if your top priorities are minimal ingredients, value, and everyday convenience. Choose Good Culture Organic 4% if you also prioritize certified organic ingredients and pasture-raised dairy. Choose Kalona SuperNatural if you particularly value grass-fed milk and traditional processing methods. For your stated preference, I'd buy Daisy 4% first. Its three-ingredient formula is exactly what I'd look for, and you don't need to pay an organic premium just to avoid additives.
