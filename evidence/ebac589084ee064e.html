For a simple ingredient profile, the best cottage cheese options in the United States come down to Daisy and Good Culture. Conventional cottage cheese brands often rely on added gums, chemical preservatives, carrageenan, or starches to thicken their product, but these two brands skip the stabilizers entirely in favor of clean dairy. The Ultra-Minimalist Standard: Daisy If your absolute top priority is the fewest ingredients possible, Daisy Pure & Natural Cottage Cheese is the benchmark choice.  The Ingredients: Cultured skim milk, cream, and salt—plus live and active cultures. That is it. Why Choose It: It contains zero stabilizers or gums. Instead of using starches to bind the liquid, Daisy relies on a proprietary cultured dressing that coats the curds naturally. The result is a clean, classic flavor profile that acts much like a savory sour cream with firm, uniform curds. It is widely available at standard grocery retailers nationwide like Walmart and Target.  The Premium Probiotic Option: Good Culture If you want clean ingredients paired with an focus on organic farming and gut health, Good Culture Cottage Cheese is heavily favored by reviewers and nutritionists.  The Ingredients: Organic skim milk, organic whole milk, organic cream, Celtic sea salt, and live and active cultures. Why Choose It: Like Daisy, Good Culture contains no chemical preservatives, gums (like guar or locust bean gum), or stabilizers. They elevate their recipe by using mineral-rich Celtic sea salt and sourcing their milk from pasture-raised cows on family farms. It has a uniquely thick, rich consistency with minimal liquid pooling at the top.
