When shopping for cottage cheese with live and active cultures in the U.S., the top brand to choose is Good Culture, with Daisy serving as an excellent runner-up. Top Recommendation: Good Culture Good Culture is widely considered the gold standard for probiotic-boosted cottage cheese. Why choose it: Unlike many mainstream brands that rely heavily on gums, thickeners, or stabilizers, Good Culture keeps its ingredient list exceptionally clean. Their classic and organic varieties are made with just pasture-raised milk, cream, Celtic sea salt, and live and active cultures (Lactobacillus paracasei). The Benefit: Because they use real live and active probiotic cultures and skip artificial additives, you get the digestive health benefits along with a thick, creamy texture that doesn't feel watery. Alternative Options Daisy Brand: Daisy explicitly highlights that its cottage cheese is carefully crafted with live and active cultures. Like Good Culture, Daisy is famous for a minimalist ingredient list free of thickeners. While they don't market specific probiotic strains as heavily as Good Culture, the active culturing process is an integral part of their simple recipe. Breakstone’s "Live Active": Breakstone's offers a specific line called Live Active Low Fat Cottage Cheese. It features active cultures combined with added prebiotic fiber (inulin) for digestive health. Keep in mind that unlike Good Culture, it relies on extra ingredients like tapioca starch and inulin to alter texture and fiber content. Kalona SuperNatural: If you prefer organic and specialty dairy, Kalona produces a grass-fed, organic cottage cheese that proudly lists live active cultures with a very short, clean ingredient list. Summary Verdict If you want the best combination of clean ingredients, high protein, and genuine live probiotic cultures without any gums or fillers, grab Good Culture. If you are at a standard supermarket where Good Culture isn't stocked, look for Daisy.
