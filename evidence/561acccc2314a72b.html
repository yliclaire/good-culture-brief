My top recommendation is Friendship Dairies No Salt Added Cottage Cheese. It has just 45 mg of sodium per ½-cup serving, compared with roughly 280–400 mg in many regular cottage cheeses. It also delivers 16 grams of protein per serving, making it an excellent choice when you're trying to reduce sodium without sacrificing protein. What to look for on the label 140 mg sodium or less per serving: Meets the FDA definition of low sodium. No salt added: A useful phrase, but always check sodium content because dairy naturally contains some sodium. Low fat isn't necessarily low sodium: A 1% or 2% cottage cheese may still contain several hundred milligrams of sodium. The FDA recommends that most adults consume less than 2,300 mg of sodium daily; individual medical recommendations may be lower. Bottom line: Buy Friendship Dairies No Salt Added if you can find it. Hood No Salt Added is a strong second choice. Both keep sodium very low while providing plenty of protein, and you can add flavor with fruit, herbs, pepper, or salt-free seasonings.
