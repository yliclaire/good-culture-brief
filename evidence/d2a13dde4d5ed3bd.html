Live and active cultures means the food contains living microorganisms. Probiotics are live microorganisms that provide a health benefit when consumed in adequate amounts. The specific bacterial strain , quantity of viable organisms, and clinical evidence matter more than simply the number of cultures listed. Nancy's specifically identifies L. acidophilus and B. lactis , giving it an advantage in transparency. However, species names alone don't establish a clinically proven benefit. None of these three brands should automatically be assumed superior for gut health without strain-specific and viable-count information.
