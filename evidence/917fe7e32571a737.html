Reviewed answer

Buy Great Value 4% Milkfat Small Curd Cottage Cheese (24 oz) from Walmart if you want the lowest cost per serving. It costs about $2.87–$2.94 per 24-oz tub, or 12.0–12.3 cents per ounce—slightly cheaper than Aldi’s comparable Friendly Farms tub. Why Great Value wins on cost Using the standard 1/2-cup (about 4 oz) serving, a 24-oz tub provides roughly six servings, so it works out to about $0.48–$0.49 per serving. The 4% version has 12 g of protein per serving, matching Aldi’s regular small-curd option. If you prefer lower fat: Great Value 1% or 2% low-fat small curd is the same 24-oz price, so it does not improve cost per serving but may fit your nutrition preference. If Aldi is more convenient: Friendly Farms 4% small curd is an excellent close second at 13¢/oz and 12 g protein per serving. Caveat Prices vary by store and region, and Walmart’s online price may differ from your local [name and location withheld] store. The practical rule: compare the price per ounce on the shelf tag and choose the largest plain tub with the lowest unit price; avoid single-serve cups, which usually cost substantially more per ounce.
