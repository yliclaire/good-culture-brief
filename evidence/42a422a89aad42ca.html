Reviewed answer

Choose Good Culture 2% Milkfat Simply Cottage Cheese if your top priority is a short, clean ingredient list. Its Simply line is made with skim milk, whole milk, cream, sea salt, and live active cultures—no gums, thickeners, added sugar, or artificial ingredients. Why Good Culture Minimal ingredients: Milk, cream, sea salt, and live cultures are exactly the kind of short list to look for in cottage cheese. No common stabilizers: Many cottage cheeses add gums or thickeners such as guar gum, locust bean gum, or carrageenan; Good Culture’s Simply version avoids them. Good texture and protein: Reviewers consistently describe it as thick, creamy, and high in protein—about 19 g per serving in one review. Widely recommended: Multiple chef and expert roundups name Good Culture their top pick specifically for its simple ingredients and clean, creamy profile. Best alternative If you want the shortest possible list and a lower price, choose Daisy Cottage Cheese. Its 4% variety uses just milk, cream, and salt, with no stabilizers or thickeners; Consumer Reports also rated its 2% small-curd version among its top performers. Priority Best pick Why Simplest overall formula + creamy texture Good Culture Simply 2% Milk, cream, sea salt, live cultures; no gums or artificial ingredients. Fewest ingredients / budget-friendly Daisy Milk, cream, and salt; no stabilizers or thickeners. Organic preference Good Culture Organic 2% Organic dairy with a similarly simple formulation, though its texture is denser. For your preference, I’d start with Good Culture Simply 2% Milkfat Classic. If you find it too thick or expensive, Daisy 2% or 4% is the strongest simple-ingredient backup.
