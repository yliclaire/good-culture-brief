Buy Walmart’s Great Value cottage cheese—specifically the 24 oz 4% small-curd tub if you want the lowest cost per serving. At about $2.87–$2.94 for 24 oz, it costs roughly 12.0–12.3 cents per ounce, making it materially cheaper than Kroger, Target’s Good & Gather, and Daisy. Why it wins on cost Brand / product Typical price Cost per oz Relative cost Great Value 4% Small Curd, 24 oz $2.87–$2.94 12.0–12.3¢ Lowest Kroger 4% Small Curd, 24 oz $3.19 13.3¢ ~8–11% more Good & Gather 4% Small Curd, 24 oz $3.39 14.1¢ ~15–18% more Daisy 4%, 24 oz $3.94–$5.29 16.4–22.0¢ ~33–83% more Cottage cheese nutrition labels commonly use a 1/2-cup serving, so a 24 oz tub provides about 6 servings. That puts Great Value at roughly 48–49 cents per serving, versus about 53 cents for Kroger, 57 cents for Good & Gather, and 66–88 cents for Daisy. Which version to choose Best value: Great Value 4% small curd, 24 oz—lowest price and the richest, most conventional cottage-cheese style. If you prefer lower fat: Great Value 2% low-fat, also $2.94 for 24 oz, has the same unit price in the listed Walmart listing. If you want fat-free: Great Value 0% is listed at the same $2.87 / 12.0¢ per oz price, though taste and texture may be less satisfying. Caveats Prices vary by store and region, and Walmart’s online price may differ from your local [name and location withheld]-area store. But based on current listed prices, Great Value is the clear cost-per-serving winner; Kroger’s store brand is the best fallback if you shop at Kroger and its local price is competitive. If taste matters more than a few cents per serving, Daisy is often preferred for its simple ingredient list, but it costs substantially more per ounce.
