No gums or starches. $4.46 · Walmart (16 oz) What to look for on the label The ideal cottage cheese ingredient list is simple: Look for: Milk, cream, salt, and cultures. Optional: Rennet or enzymes used in cheesemaking. Less necessary: Carrageenan, guar gum, xanthan gum, modified food starch, and added sweeteners. Gums and stabilizers aren't automatically harmful; they're often used to improve texture and prevent separation. They simply aren't needed if your priority is minimal ingredients. One nutritional consideration: even clean-label cottage cheese can contain substantial sodium. For example, Good Culture Organic 4% has 380 mg per half-cup serving. My final pick: Buy Daisy 4% for everyday use. Choose Good Culture Organic 4% if organic sourcing is important to you. Both fit your preference for straightforward ingredients, and there's no compelling reason to pay more solely to get a shorter label. Always double-check the specific container's ingredients, since formulations can change.
