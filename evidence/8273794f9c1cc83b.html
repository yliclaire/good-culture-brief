If you dislike lumpy textures, I recommend trying whipped cottage cheese first, rather than regular cottage cheese. Whipped varieties are designed to be smoother, with a consistency closer to thick Greek yogurt or a creamy spread. If you can't find a whipped version, my top choice is Good Culture 4% Whole Milk Cottage Cheese. The trick for a completely smooth texture Even small-curd cottage cheese has noticeable lumps. For a truly smooth result, take Good Culture 4% and blend it in a food processor or blender until creamy, usually around 30–60 seconds. You'll get something closer to whipped ricotta or thick Greek yogurt, while retaining cottage cheese's high protein content. My final pick: Buy Good Culture 4% Whole Milk Cottage Cheese if you want an easy-to-find starting point. Try it as-is, and blend it if the small curds still bother you. If you find Anderson Erickson Whipped Smooth locally, try that first for the least preparation.
