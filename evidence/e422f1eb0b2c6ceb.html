My top pick is Good Culture 2% Milkfat Simply Cottage Cheese for a high-protein snack: it delivers about 14 g of protein per 1/2-cup serving while keeping the ingredient list simple, and it is widely available in U.S. grocery stores. Why Good Culture Strong protein density: 14 g per half cup makes it an easy way to add protein without a large snack. Simple formulation: Its “Simply” line is a good fit if you prefer fewer additives than some flavored or high-protein cottage cheeses. Balanced texture and taste: It has also performed well in taste comparisons, which matters if you plan to eat it plain or with fruit. Good alternatives Brand Best for Protein noted Good Culture 2% Simply Overall high-protein everyday snack 14 g per 1/2 cup Daisy 4% Classic taste and simple ingredients 13 g per 1/2 cup Clover Sonoma Organic Organic preference 14 g per 1/2 cup Breakstone’s Live Active Low-Fat Portable single-serve cups 12 g per serving Practical choice For the highest protein per bite, choose Good Culture 2% Simply; for the most convenient grab-and-go option, choose Breakstone’s single-serve cups. If sodium is a concern, compare labels because cottage cheese can be relatively salty, and consider a no-salt-added variety such as Friendship Dairies 1% Small Curd, which lists 15 g protein with much lower sodium per serving.
