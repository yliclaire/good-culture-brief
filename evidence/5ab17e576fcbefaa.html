Good Culture 2% Simply Cottage Cheese is my top pick for a high-protein snack in the U.S. It delivers about 14 g of protein per 1/2-cup serving, uses a short, simple ingredient list, and has a thick, creamy texture that makes it satisfying on its own or with fruit and nuts. Why it stands out Protein density: At roughly 14 g per half cup, it is among the higher-protein mainstream cottage cheeses, comparable to other strong options such as Clover Sonoma. Simple formulation: Good Culture’s “Simply” line is typically made with cultured milk, cream, salt, and live active cultures—no gums, starches, or added sugars—so you get protein without unnecessary additives. Snack-friendly texture: Its rich, small-curd style works well eaten straight from the container, spread on toast, or paired with berries; reviewers have also rated it highly for flavor and texture. Widely available: It is sold in many U.S. supermarkets, making it a practical repeat purchase. If you prefer the richest flavor and don’t mind a bit more fat, Daisy 4% is a very solid, widely available choice.
