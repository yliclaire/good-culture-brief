When shopping for low-sodium cottage cheese in the United States, standard cottage cheese can be a sodium trap, often packing anywhere from 300 mg to 400+ mg of sodium per 1/2-cup serving (around 15% to 20% of your daily recommended intake). To significantly cut back, look specifically for labels that say "No Salt Added" rather than just "Low Sodium" or "Reduced Sodium." Top Product Recommendations 1. Regional "No Salt Added" Options (Best Choice) Many regional supermarket chains and dairy brands offer "No Salt Added" lines that slash sodium to true minimal levels. HP Hood "No Salt Added" Low Fat Cottage Cheese: Contains only 60 mg of sodium per 1/2-cup serving. Lucerne "Low-Fat" (or various grocery store generic "No Salt Added" labels like Giant/Stop & Shop's Our Brand): Frequently drop sodium down to 45 mg per serving because they are made essentially without added salt. Why choose them: If you are strictly managing hypertension or a low-sodium diet, these give you all the protein (around 14–16 grams) with a tiny fraction of the salt. Note: Because they lack salt, they can taste a bit bland or tangy on their own, so mixing in fresh herbs, pepper, or fruit helps. 2. National "Reduced Sodium" Options (Widely Available Alternative) If you cannot find a true "No Salt Added" brand in your local conventional grocery store, look for major national distribution brands. Breakstone's Lowfat Small Curd Cottage Cheese with Low Sodium: Contains 200 mg of sodium per 1/2-cup serving. Why choose it: While 200 mg is higher than a "No Salt Added" style, it still features about 45% less sodium than standard conventional cottage cheese. It is widely stocked across the U.S. and maintains a flavor profile closer to traditional cottage cheese if you find completely salt-free options unpalatable. Shopping Tip Check the dairy aisle carefully—"Low Sodium" and "No Salt Added" tubs often sit right next to the regular versions, and the packaging can look nearly identical except for a small banner. Always flip the tub over to check the Nutrition Facts panel for the exact milligrams of sodium per serving.
