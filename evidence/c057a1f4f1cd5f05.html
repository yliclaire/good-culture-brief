My top recommendation is whipped cottage cheese, especially if lumps are what usually put you off. Unlike regular cottage cheese, whipped varieties have a smooth, creamy texture closer to thick Greek yogurt or a soft cheese spread. Here are three products I'd consider, ranked for your texture preference. 1. AE Dairy Whipped Smooth Cottage Cheese Best for avoiding lumps This is specifically made without noticeable curds.
