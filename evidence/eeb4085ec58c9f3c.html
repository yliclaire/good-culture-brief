It has just 45 mg of sodium per ½-cup serving, while many regular cottage cheeses contain 300–400 mg or more. That makes it an excellent choice if reducing sodium is your main priority, without sacrificing protein. What to look for when shopping Choose "No Salt Added" when possible. Naturally occurring sodium remains, but added salt is omitted. Check the Nutrition Facts label. In U.S. labeling, "low sodium" generally means 140 mg or less per serving. "Reduced sodium" can still mean substantially more. Don't confuse low-fat with low-sodium. A fat-free or 1% cottage cheese can still contain lots of salt. Consider taste. No-salt-added cottage cheese may taste milder. Fresh berries, cinnamon, herbs, or salt-free seasoning can help. Friendship's no-salt-added variety may be harder to find, so check participating Whole Foods locations or other grocers, and confirm the exact package label. Bottom line: Choose Friendship Dairies No Salt Added 1% if you can find it. At 45 mg sodium and 16 g protein per ½ cup, it's the strongest sodium-conscious option among the products I verified. Breakstone's Low Sodium is a useful fallback at 200 mg, although it does not meet the FDA's strict "low sodium" threshold.
