If you prefer simple ingredients, the two standout cottage cheese brands in the United States are Daisy and Good Culture (specifically their "Simply" line). Food Network Many conventional commercial cottage cheeses rely heavily on thickeners, gums (like guar gum or locust bean gum), starches, and preservatives to maintain texture. Daisy and Good Culture largely avoid these additives. Top Brand Recommendations 1. Daisy Cottage Cheese (The Cleanest Mainstream Option) Ingredients: Cultured skim milk, cream, salt, and enzymes. Why choose it: Daisy is widely available across almost all major grocery stores nationwide and is famous for its literal four-ingredient label. It contains no gums, no stabilizers, and no starches. The creamy texture comes naturally from real milk and cream, giving it a clean, fresh, and slightly tart dairy flavor. 2. Good Culture "Simply" Cottage Cheese (The Probiotic Upgrade) Ingredients: Skim milk, whole milk, cream, sea salt, and live and active cultures. Why choose it: While Good Culture's standard classic line uses a tiny bit of thickener (organic guar gum and locust bean gum), their "Simply" line completely strips those away, resulting in a clean, gum-free ingredient deck. Additionally, it contains live and active probiotic cultures, features a thick and velvety texture, and uses pasture-raised dairy when possible. Comparison at a Glance Feature Daisy Cottage Cheese Good Culture "Simply" Ingredient List 4 simple ingredients (Cultured skim milk, cream, salt, enzymes) 5 ingredients (Skim milk, whole milk, cream, sea salt, live cultures) Gums / Thickeners None None Probiotics Standard dairy cultures Contains live and active probiotic cultures Availability Ubiquitous nationwide Widely available (Target, Whole Foods, Kroger, Publix, etc.) Summary Recommendation Choose Daisy if you want the absolute simplest, most widely accessible 4-ingredient product on the shelf. Choose Good Culture ("Simply" variant) if you want a gum-free ingredient list paired with added live probiotics and an exceptionally thick, rich curd texture.
