Buy Good Culture Simply 4% Milkfat Classic Cottage Cheese if you want the best-tasting, most versatile option. It earned Wirecutter’s top pick for its thick, creamy curds, balanced salt and milky sweetness, and clean flavor that works sweet or savory. Best choice Good Culture’s 4% version has fine, plush curds and a rich but not gloppy texture; its 2% low-fat version is also excellent if you want fewer calories without sacrificing creaminess. Consumer Reports similarly singled out Good Culture’s 2% organic version as the best cottage cheese for spreading because of its thick, ricotta-like texture. Pick by preference If you want… Buy Why Best overall flavor and texture Good Culture Simply 4% Thick, creamy, well-balanced, and versatile with toppings. Lower fat but still rich Good Culture Simply 2% Surprisingly thick and luxurious for a low-fat cottage cheese. Widely available, simple ingredients Daisy Low-Fat 2% Small Curd Consumer Reports’ top overall pick, with a simple ingredient list, mild sour-cream-like tang, and firm curds. Tangy, dense, classic style Friendship Dairies 4% Small Curd Fine, ricotta-like curds with buttermilk-like tang; excellent in recipes. Budget option Aldi Friendly Farms Regular Small Curd Larger, firmer curds and the lowest cost among Wirecutter’s picks, at about 13 cents per ounce. No added salt Friendship Dairies 1% No Salt Added A solid low-sodium choice, though it will taste less lively without salt. What to check Milkfat: Choose 4% for the richest, most spoonable result; 2% is a good middle ground. Consumer Reports notes that full-fat has about 3 g saturated fat per half cup versus roughly 1.5 g for 2%. Ingredients: For the cleanest product, look for milk, cream, salt, and live active cultures; thickeners such as guar gum, locust bean gum, tapioca starch, or carrageenan are common but not necessary. Sodium: Cottage cheese can exceed 400 mg sodium per half cup, so check the label if you eat it often or limit sodium. For a first tub in [name and location withheld], I’d try Good Culture Simply 4%; if your store is out, Daisy 2% is the most reliable widely available alternative.
