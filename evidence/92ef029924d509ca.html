Live and active cultures means viable microorganisms are present in the product. Probiotics are live microorganisms that have demonstrated a health benefit when consumed in adequate amounts. This means a product can contain live cultures without there being clinical evidence that those particular cultures improve digestive health. The exact strain and amount can matter, and not all brands disclose them. Nancy's stands out because it specifically identifies additional probiotic species in its cottage cheese. That makes it a particularly interesting option if live bacteria are your top priority, although it doesn't prove superior health benefits over the other brands. One final shopping tip: check the actual container for its live-culture claim, because formulations and labels can change. And keep it refrigerated—heating it substantially can kill the live bacteria.
