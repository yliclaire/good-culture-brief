Buy Aldi Friendly Farms Regular Small Curd Cottage Cheese if you have an Aldi nearby. It is the clearest cost-per-serving winner among recent U.S. reviews: a 24-ounce tub costs about $3, or 13 cents per ounce—roughly 52 cents per half-cup serving—and Wirecutter identifies it as the least expensive of its recommended picks. Why it wins on value Option Approx. price Cost per ounce Cost per 1/2-cup serving* Aldi Friendly Farms Regular Small Curd $3 / 24 oz 13¢ ~52¢ Roundy’s 2% 14¢/oz 14¢ ~56¢ Kemps 2% 16¢/oz 16¢ ~64¢ Good Culture 2% 28¢/oz 28¢ ~$1.12 *Assumes a standard half-cup serving, about 4 ounces. For someone eating it regularly, that price gap compounds: choosing Aldi over Good Culture saves roughly 60 cents per serving, or about $4.20 per week at one serving daily. What to buy Aldi Friendly Farms Regular Small Curd Cottage Cheese, 24 oz — best default for lowest cost per serving; it also has a dairy-forward, slightly sharp flavor that works well with both savory and sweet toppings. If you don’t have Aldi: choose your supermarket’s plain store brand—such as Kroger, Walmart Great Value, or Roundy’s—when its per-ounce price is below the national brands. Store brands are commonly the value leaders; Roundy’s, for example, was 14 cents per ounce in a recent comparison. Avoid paying for premium branding if cost is the priority: Good Culture is well reviewed for taste and protein, but at about 28 cents per ounce it costs more than twice as much per serving as Aldi. Practical buying rule Compare the shelf price divided by ounces, not the tub price. A larger tub is often cheaper per ounce, but only buy a size you will finish before it spoils. Choose plain cottage cheese rather than flavored varieties, and favor 2% or 4% milkfat if satiety matters; nonfat versions generally have less protein and may be less filling. clevelandclinic
