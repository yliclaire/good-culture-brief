Good Culture is worth the extra cost when you want tangy flavor, small curds, organic ingredients, and probiotics, while Daisy is the better choice for budget-friendly everyday use, larger curds, and a clean, mildly sweet taste. ⭐ Quick Take Good Culture : Creamiest, tangiest, small-curd winner in taste tests; organic; added probiotics; about $6.99 for 16 oz . Daisy : Runner-up with a “creamy sweetness,” large curds, minimalist ingredients, and strong value at $3.99 for 16 oz —roughly $3 cheaper . 🥄 How They Taste Good Culture was the top-rated cottage cheese in a 22‑brand tasting, praised for being creamy , lightly salted , a bit tangy , and having small curds that make it great for casual eating . Daisy was a close runner-up, described as having noticeably larger curds and a creamy sweetness that many tasters loved . 🧪 Ingredients & Nutrition Good Culture uses organic milk, cream, sea salt, and live cultures (probiotics) . Daisy uses just cultured skim milk, cream, and salt—no gums or stabilizers . Both offer high protein (around 13–14g per serving) . 💵 Price & Value Good Culture’s organic milk and probiotics push it into the premium tier. Daisy delivers excellent quality at a significantly lower price, making it one of the strongest value picks in the dairy aisle . ✅ When to Choose Good Culture You prefer small curds and a tangier , more complex flavor. You want organic dairy or value probiotics . You eat cottage cheese plain , as a snack, or in bowls where flavor and texture matter most. You’re willing to pay extra for premium taste and ingredients. ✅ When to Choose Daisy You want large curds with a creamy, lightly sweet profile. You’re shopping on a budget —it’s about $3 cheaper per tub . You use cottage cheese in recipes (pancakes, lasagna, dips) where subtle differences matter less. You prefer a clean, simple ingredient list without added cultures. 🧭 Bottom Line If cottage cheese is a daily snack and you care about flavor nuance, Good Culture earns its premium. If you want dependable quality at a great price—or prefer larger curds—Daisy is the smarter buy.
