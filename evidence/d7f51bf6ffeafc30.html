To minimize your cost per serving while buying cottage cheese regularly in the U.S., your best strategy is to look past name brands and focus on large-format private label (store brand) tubs, specifically Walmart’s Great Value or Aldi's Friendly Farms. The Winner: Walmart's Great Value Cottage Cheese (48 oz Tub) Approximate Price: ~$5.46 for 48 oz (roughly 11.4¢ per ounce) Why it wins: Volume Discount: Buying the massive 48-ounce economy tub drops the per-ounce price lower than standard 16 oz or 24 oz containers. Baseline Cost: Major national brands like Daisy or Good Culture typically cost anywhere from 20¢ to 35¢+ per ounce, meaning store brands can save you nearly 50% over the course of regular purchases. Consistency: Great Value cottage cheese is manufactured by major regional dairy suppliers, matching the nutrition profile (around 13g–14g of protein per 1/2 cup serving) and basic taste of name brands without the marketing markup. Alternative Budget Contender: Aldi (Friendly Farms) If you shop at Aldi, their Friendly Farms 24 oz or larger tubs routinely price out around 11¢ per ounce, rivaling Walmart dollar-for-dollar. Key Shopping Tips to Keep Costs Low Size Up: Avoid single-serving cups (which often cost 25¢–35¢+ per ounce) and 16 oz tubs. Stick to 24 oz or 48 oz containers for the lowest unit price. Watch Out for "Add-Ins": Avoid flavored varieties or those packaged with fruit toppings on the side, as these carry a heavy convenience tax. Buy plain store-brand tubs and add your own fruit or seasoning at home. Compare Regional Supermarkets: Other large grocery chains (like Kroger, Target, or HEB) price their store-brand 24 oz tubs competitively (usually around 13¢–14¢ per ounce), but Walmart and Aldi consistently hold the absolute floor for nationwide baseline pricing.
